# 临书 (tmpbook)

I'm an architect interested in turning complex, experience-driven problems into systems that can be understood, tested, and improved.

## What I explore

- **AI-assisted engineering:** connecting code analysis, runtime profiling, and experiments to find and validate performance improvements.
- **Engineering workflows:** making responsibilities, collaboration, and quality checks clearer so teams can deliver with greater ownership.
- **Tools for better decisions:** translating ideas into practical rules, feedback loops, and small tools.

## Engineering in the AI era

AI makes it easier to work across disciplines. I believe this gives engineers room to take ownership beyond implementation: clarifying requirements and interaction details, building the solution, and validating the result. Resolving uncertainty earlier can reduce handoffs and improve delivery quality.

## How I work

I like to make implicit knowledge explicit, question assumptions, and distinguish a plausible explanation from evidence. A useful system should be practical enough to use, observable enough to learn from, and simple enough to maintain.
