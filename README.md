# 临书 (tmpbook)

I'm an architect and lifelong learner interested in turning complex, experience-driven problems into systems that can be understood, tested, and improved.

## What I explore

- **AI-assisted engineering:** connecting code analysis, runtime profiling, and experiments to find and validate performance improvements.
- **Engineering workflows:** making responsibilities, collaboration, and quality checks clearer so teams can deliver with greater ownership.
- **Tools for better decisions:** translating ideas into practical rules, feedback loops, and small tools.

## Engineering in the AI era

AI is changing the boundaries of engineering roles. As it lowers the cost of working across disciplines, engineers can take broader ownership—from interaction design and implementation to validation. By resolving details earlier and taking responsibility through to delivery, teams can reduce handoffs and focus on outcomes.

## How I work

I like to make implicit knowledge explicit, question assumptions, distinguish a plausible explanation from evidence, and simplify where I can. A useful system should be practical enough to use, observable enough to learn from, and simple enough to maintain.
